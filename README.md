A queue explained to a 10-year-old in two lines: a human caterpillar, and an honest account of what the analogy maps and what it leaves out. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run" bounty, $2 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes two lines to `/output/entry.txt`:

> A queue is a human caterpillar: a long line of people where each person is one little leg, and the whole creature only shuffles forward when the leg at the very front takes a step.
>
> It maps the turn-taking and the slow shuffle forward, but it leaves out the boredom, the phone-checking, and the people who try to sneak ahead.
